About

Engineering Focus & Practical Principles

I approach engineering problems by working from fundamental physical circuits upward to high-level software abstractions. Rather than treating hardware as an uninspected black box or software as isolated from physical constraints, my work focuses on building verifiable, well-characterized systems where every layer is analyzed, documented, and tested against its operating boundaries.

My engineering scope covers schematic capture and PCB layout in KiCad, switched-mode power electronics, analog signal generation, embedded microcontroller firmware for ARM Cortex-M and ESP32 architectures, deterministic wireless telemetry, frequency-domain vibration signal processing, and defensive software systems.

Detailed technical architectures, circuit schematics, source code, and comprehensive audit reports are maintained in the respective project repositories, while this portfolio offers a structured overview of the scope and outcomes.

Methodology

Practical Engineering Workflow

A disciplined approach to system design, validation, and transparent technical documentation across physical hardware and software.

01

Mathematical Calculations & Modeling

Deriving circuit parameters (duty cycle, ripple currents, resonant tank tuning, power dissipation) and algorithmic formulas before hardware layout or code execution.

02

Schematic Capture & Simulation

Constructing hierarchical schematics in KiCad, running virtual simulations in Tinkercad or SPICE, and performing electrical rule checks prior to physical layout.

03

Physical Layout & Fabrication

Designing single and 2-layer PCBs with clearance audits, generating standardized Gerbers/BOMs, or executing manual FeCl3 chemical etching for rapid bench prototyping.

04

Firmware Architecture & Protocols

Developing embedded C/C++ firmware with hardware register configuration (timers, ADC, interrupts), non-blocking execution loops, and robust communication buses (I2C, SPI, UART, BLE, ESP-NOW).

05

Signal Processing & Software Backends

Implementing mathematical signal transforms (FFT, Hann windowing, statistical moments) and designing decoupled REST/WebSocket backends with clean data validation.

06

Verification Audits & Limitations Disclosure

Writing automated test suites (Python, Arduino CLI), documenting toolchain constraints, identifying failure modes, and clearly separating simulated prototypes from production systems.
